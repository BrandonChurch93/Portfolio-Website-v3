What each shows:

- `light-trail-1.jpg`: a lamp swung on a string. Soft, wide ribbons of light that cross over each other on black. Neutral white, not warm.
- `light-trail-2.jpg`: a pendulum light painting. Thin, sharp, even lines of light looping on black. Grayscale.
- `light-trail-3.jpg`: the warm one. Glowing filament wires inside a bulb, amber-gold, white-hot where the wire is thinnest.
- `gold-leaf-1.jpg`: crumpled gold leaf, close up. Faceted creases, some catching the light hard, some going amber and translucent. No copper leaf was found that was free to use; gold is the stand-in.
- `gold-leaf-2.jpg`: loose torn flakes of gold leaf on a pale ground. The torn, ragged edges and pinholes are the thing to look at.
- `glass-shards-1.jpg`: a cracked pane on a dark ground. The crack lines catch the light as bright, beaded threads.
- `glass-shards-2.jpg`: a wine glass shattering, shards flying. The thin edges of each shard pick up a line of light. Daylight and a busy background.
- `glass-caustics-1.jpg`: sunlight through a glass bottle onto a surface. Warm caustic curves with bright cusps.
- `grinder-sparks-1.jpg`: an angle grinder on steel. A long fan of straight orange streaks, a few bursting at the ends.
- `grinder-sparks-2.jpg`: an angle grinder, closer. Sparks wrap round the disc then fly out straight. Many short streaks fork.
- `sparkler-1.jpg`: a sparkler head on, with branching sparks that fork into small stars.
- `sparkler-2.jpg`: a sparkler in grass at night, with short sparks and soft out-of-focus points.
- `arc-1.jpg`: Tesla coil streamers on a dark ground. Branching, jagged violet-white threads.
- `arc-2.jpg`: a Jacob's ladder arc between two electrodes. The camera's focus is soft, but it shows the violet edge and the hot white body of the arc.
