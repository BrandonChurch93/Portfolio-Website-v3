# The pass over the whole site: what was found

His word, the night of Nov 15 to 16: "do one more pass and go through the whole entire website section by section. figure out where you can make improvements on the 3D assets, overall user experience, animations, user interface, micro interactions". `BRIEF.md`, Settled, Hero, "After the hero".

This is the finding stage only: nothing in it has been changed yet. One critic looked at each section against `QUALITY.md`, and a second reader checked every proposal against the code and against `BRIEF.md`. What is made, and what he is asked about, is recorded in `LOG.md` as it happens. `shots/` is not committed, so the stills it names are on the machine that took them.

- Notes and every still are in `shots/pass/` (`work-notes.md`, `skills-notes.md`, `about-notes.md`, `contact-notes.md`, `whole-notes.md`).
- Judged on hardware at 1440x900, 1920x1080 and an iPad held sideways (1366x1024, touch), on each section's `/lab` page and in place on `/lab/whole?ui=0`.
- A second reader checked every item against the code and against `BRIEF.md`.
- "Can be made now" goes against no ruling. "Needs his word first" goes against a ruling, or comes close to one, and must not be built until he says yes.
- Tags read **[lens · size · worth]**. Worth runs from 1 to 5.
