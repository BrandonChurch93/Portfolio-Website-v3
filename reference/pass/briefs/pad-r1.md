## Do not touch (the critic's "strongest", and his rulings)
The tower's form. The screen seated for the landing. The rocket coming back down on its fire when the page is scrolled up. The order of things: the stream lands on the stone's outer band, the stone lights band by band from its rim to its middle on the clock, the rocket launches when the boss it stands on lights. Nothing of the pad goes dark while the rocket is in the air.

## What to make, most important first (each from reference/pass/findings.md, Contact, "Can be made now")
1. THE ROCKET'S FIRE (parts/rocket/fire.ts, PLUME, the core and diamonds in FRAG). Now: a soft orange smear ending in a rounded blob. The core and the diamonds vanish at the page's size, and its length is fixed. Wanted: a pale hard throat under the bell, shock diamonds a visitor can count, flame tongues at its edges, a tail that grows past the foot of the screen as the rocket climbs. In the stream's colors only (item 22): ember, flame, hot, pale, never white, never blue. No smoke. No soft round ball of glow anywhere (QUALITY.md fails it on sight).
2. THE STONE BEFORE AND DURING THE FILL (parts/stone/material.ts). Now: the unlit stone already reads as bright copper, so fill 0, 0.5 and 1 look nearly alike and the landing barely reads. Wanted: the stone nearly dark before the landing (the room and the cursor's light only, as item 17 asks), and each ring's lighting an EVENT: it comes on, overshoots a little and settles, ring after ring up to the boss.
3. THE ROCKET LIGHTS FROM ITS FOOT. Now: only two portholes light. Wanted: its bands and seams light in order from its foot as the stone fills (FUEL in layout.ts says how full the stone is for each part), so a visitor sees it being readied.
4. THE STONE'S MIDDLE AT IGNITION. Now: from ignition to lift it is a smeared orange wash and the carving is lost. Wanted: the carving lit hot and still readable, the cuts brighter and the tops darker, the fire on the stone kept to tongues at the table's edge (item 17).
5. THE ROCKET'S BODY AND FINS (parts/rocket/model.ts, form.ts). Now: fins that are flat one tone plates with no thickness or root, a body mostly unbroken gloss black. Wanted: fins with thickness and a fairing where they join, faces that turn to the light differently, panel seams and hatches that catch the cursor's light. It is a large item: do it last, and only if 1 to 4 are right.
6. THE TOWER'S ARMS (parts/tower/model.ts). Now: 0.6 s into the launch they hang tilted down and away like loose planks. Wanted: they swing back level about a hinge at the tower, ease to a stop and settle a little.
