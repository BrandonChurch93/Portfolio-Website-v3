## What must change, most important first
1. CUT CARD. Every lit face is one flat plane with a thin outline, "layered cardboard or papier mache" at the page's size and at three times. Stone that has been broken is KNAPPED: break each plate's front into 5 to 15 sub faces of 15 to 40 units, convex and concave, each at an angle of its own (5 to 25 degrees off its neighbours), meeting along straight or shell shaped (conchoidal) fracture lines that meet at angles. A plate then catches the core's light as a cluster of facets of different brightness, with a few in shade. The outlines stay on the picture's: only what is inside them is broken up. Replace the soft outlines that follow the traced raster with crisp fracture runs.
2. A RING OF TILES. The plates either side of the core are upright rectangles and strips. The picture's lean diagonally, a fan of wedges all leaning in toward the core, like the broken slabs of a crater. Keep the traced outlines and re-angle the faces and their sides.
3. THE FLOOR'S UNDERSIDE IS A BATTLEMENT at three times: vertical walls and flat topped square teeth either side of the stream (about 640 to 740 across), flat black under it, at the centre of attention. The picture's is a thick rock belly that falls away down to the right (575 to 600 down under the core, into the lit hanging mass at 860 to 920 across and 570 to 640 down), its edge ragged and chipped at every angle.
4. NO PATTERN WITH A FIXED SPACING, anywhere. Three were found: the even horizontal beds on the plates' sides, which read as brickwork or stairs (make them irregular fracture and strata of uneven thickness that pinch out). The comb of teeth at the left and right ends of the roof lid. And the regular sawtooth of both torn lines, which the skin's reviewers saw too as "a regular sawtooth of square steps": trace both torn lines again as an irregular break (bites of very different sizes, some long straight runs of 30 to 60 pixels at odd angles, some tight chatter), still on the picture's own torn outline. This moves the torn lines: say so in the first line of your report, with the file the skin's builder reads, so the skin is made again against them.
5. THE ROOF. The picture has a thick broken stratum under the land: a pale, moonlit front face from about 268 to 330 down at the middle, over dark hanging slabs whose lower edges catch the core. Yours is the land's thin edge over a dark band and a grey lid like papier mache. Build that stratum as heavy, sharp edged broken blocks. Its front face takes the moon: the moon is lower now (MOONWARD in layout.ts), behind and to the right.
6. THE FRAME is to be near black with a few lit edges, and its darks a nearly neutral brown. With the fill at 0.08 most of that is done for you: check it, and put the lit edges where the picture has them.
