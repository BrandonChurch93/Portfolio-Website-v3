# Judging the pyramid

What is being judged: the gold pyramid at the front left of the hero's world. Its place and size are picture 9's (reference/concept/09-parts/09b-the-pyramid.jpg, and the whole picture: apex near 355 across, 185 down). Its LOOK is not the picture's: the picture's lit face is a near white glare in a yellow gold, and this site wants neither. Brandon's rule: the look is to be better than the picture's. So judge the look by QUALITY.md and reference/kits/README.md, "The look" ("Metal reads as metal by what it reflects: long sharp reflections that slide across it. A soft orange gradient is paint."), and beside reference/bar/pc-builder-parts.jpg, not by how close it is to the picture's glare.

Stills of your own: npm run match -- --at 200,140,340,210 --out shots/world/pyramid/review-match, npm run look -- pyramid --out shots/world/pyramid/review-page.jpg, npm run look -- close:pyramid --w 1500 --h 1000 --out shots/world/pyramid/review-close.jpg, the page still at --scale 1, the same with --lamp 0.2,0.4 and --lamp 0.4,0.6 (does a glint slide?), and the hero itself: npm run shot -- "/lab/hero?ui=0" --t 6 --wait 3000 --out shots/world/pyramid/review-hero.jpg. The city, trees and land round it are other parts or plain dark stand ins.

Judge: does it sit where the picture has it? Would a stranger say gold, without being led: polished metal, or paint, satin, plastic, caramel, peach, copper? Is there light and dark with an edge inside the lit face, and things mirrored (few, quiet, sharp among soft)? Is the dark face deep satin gold? Does the surface live at three times zoom (plates, hairlines) with no brick grid? One clean pale line on the front arris and the right outline, a crisp reveal under the capstone? Is it the most beautiful object in the hero's first screen? Anything yellow, white, banded, noisy, sparkling?
