Close up each is 8 to 12 smooth round lumps of one size, every one with its own crisp arc and its own lit right edge, some of them flat discs seen face on: "cauliflower", "popcorn", "model railway clump foliage". And there is LESS to find in a crown than round 6 had. A crown is three or four LARGE clumps of very different sizes with dark gaps between them, smaller clumps inside those, and leaves at the scale round 6 had. It is lit as ONE mass: only the clumps on its top and its moon side catch the moon, the middle and the gaps fall to deep brown (store how open each lump is), and the side away from the moon is dark.
