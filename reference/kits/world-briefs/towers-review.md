# Judging the towers

What is being judged: the city's buildings on top of the hero's world. The tower with the stepped gold crown (at the right of the spire) was liked by Brandon in its first round: "the building on the right looks way better" than picture 9's small plain one (shots/world/towers/final-close-beside.jpg). It is the standard for the others, and it must not have become plainer.

References: reference/concept/09-parts/09c-the-skyline.jpg, 09d-the-stepped-gold-crown-and-the-sun-disc.jpg, the whole picture, reference/bar/pc-builder-parts.jpg, and shots/world/towers/final-alone.png (the approved tower).

Stills of your own: npm run match -- --at 60,0,1260,330 --out shots/world/towers/review-match (the skyline laid over the picture), npm run look -- crown --out shots/world/towers/review-page.jpg, npm run look -- close:crown --w 1500 --h 1000 --out shots/world/towers/review-close.jpg, npm run look -- only:towers --w 1600 --h 1000 --out shots/world/towers/review-alone.jpg, and npm run look -- whole --scale 1 --out shots/world/towers/review-whole-1x.jpg (look for sparkle and moire). Buildings that are still plain dark boxes are stand ins: count them, do not judge them.

Judge: do the built towers' tops and outlines sit where the picture has them? Is the stepped tower still the one Brandon liked, with its four faults fixed (straight reeds, gold that is gold, windows with depth close up, good light under the new moon)? Is every other built tower at its standard, or a box with windows on a grid? Are no two neighbours alike? Can you name stone, bronze, glass and gold? Is the city warm and lit, with windows in clumps? Does light land on things? Anything white, blue, green, sparkling, haloed, yellow?
