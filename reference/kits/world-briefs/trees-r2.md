## What is yours to fix, most important first
1. THE LIGHT IS WRONG FOR THIS WORLD. The crowns are lit evenly from the front, so they have no top and no underside and the gaps between them are lit. The moon is behind the world and to the viewer's right (MOONWARD). Each crown: a pale warm rim on its upper right where the moon catches it, its top paler than its sides, the face toward the viewer in a soft dim light, its underside and inside dark, and the gaps between crowns near black. Store that in the models (how open each clump is, from above and from the moon's side) and keep the night's soft frontal light on them low.
2. COLOR. The belt is one saturated orange. The picture's is russet, tan and brown with pale gold only on lit tops. Widen the spread from tree to tree so it reads at the page's size: deep bronze browns, copper, a few ember reds, a few pale gold. The pale ones came out a dull khaki (about 97, 69, 44) that reads olive: warm them. Never green.
3. THEY FLOAT. No shadow shows at the belt's foot. The land is being built by another builder and will take the moon's shadows of your crowns if your meshes cast (check they do, into the stage's shadow map). Add your own soft wide darkening on the ground under and between the crowns as well (a dark soft disc is not enough seen edge on: darken a band of ground under the belt, falling away toward the viewer's left, away from the moon).
4. BEADS ON A STRING. Crowns of one size at even spacing read as a clipped hedge. The new places help. In the models, give more difference: four to six models, tall and squat, single and twin stemmed, crowns from 0.7 to 1.3, so no two neighbours match.
5. THE TRUNK is a smooth grey cylinder with a hard step at its flare, the same black peg on every tree. Taper it, give it bark tone, run limbs up into the crown, vary its height. Two instances stood on the same spot: with the new places that should not happen, check.
6. COST. Round 1 used 198,640 triangles on 260 trees. There are 300 now and they are larger. The view never moves and most of the back rows are half hidden: give the rows behind the first a cheaper model (fewer clumps), and stay under 260,000 triangles drawn and 8 draw calls.
