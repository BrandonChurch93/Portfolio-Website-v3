## What must change, most important first
1. IT LEAVES WITH FORCE. At the page's size the neck reads as "a liquid drip", "poured honey": a flat cream plateau about 45 pixels wide with hard 3 pixel edges that thins to a thread. The kit says the stream is a beam shot out of the core, not a drip. Keep the picture's taper (it is measured: 35, 22, 17, 11, 9 wide at 0, 16, 38, 83, 128 deep), but give it the light of a beam: brightest and palest down its heart, falling through hot to a deep amber at its edges, so it has no flat plateau and no hard outline, with fine bright streaks running fast down its length on the clock (the stream's own pace: read coreFrag in src/stream/shaders.ts for how its light runs). Over its last 100 units it is the page's heart exactly: the same width, the same pale, a faint amber at its edges where the page's glow is beginning, fading out between 228 and 262.
2. THE FOOT IS A KEYHOLE. Where the neck leaves the ball the orange band hooks down into two horns round a rounded bulb. Picture 9's neck flares out of the ball in one smooth fillet with broad shoulders, and the band fades away under them. On the lab's page (where the page's stream is a plain line) the same: no hard seam under the ball.
3. BEFORE THE STREAM SETS OFF the foot is still a teat with the band notched round it like a balloon's outline. The band runs unbroken, and the bead of gathering light is INSIDE the face's light, swelling and drawing down slowly on the clock. Fable's ruling on who owns that moment: the page's resting end, with its sparks, belongs at the ball's foot then. It is the stream gathering to leave, and your bead is the light it gathers from.
4. A FEW SHARDS, PUT BACK RIGHT. Round 8 took the rock lumps off the beam, which was asked. But now the beam crosses the hollow catching nothing and then gathers a dense cloud further down. Put back a FEW (five to eight) slow heavy shards of the planet's stone round the ball and beside and behind the neck, each dark with one hot edge toward the light, none of them ever across the beam's front, none a gem or a disc: so the page's shards, which begin about 260 units down, are not the first.
5. THE FACE at three times is "terrazzo", "sandstone paper": a flat two tone mottle of straight edged patches with faint salt points, and no sense of a sphere. Facets with edges, told apart by light, smaller and denser toward the limb so the ball turns away, over the same radial profile. Clamp the glints under the pale cap: they still measure (250, 228, 17x) on 180 pixels.
