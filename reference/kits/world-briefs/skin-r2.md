## What must change, most important first
1. THE PALE SIDE'S COLOR AND LIGHT. In picture 9 the moonlit side is a pale cream beige, the brightest large thing on the planet after the core and the gold, and its craters' black crescents read at the page's size from across the room. Yours is salmon or terracotta and barely reads at the page's size. A flat patch at picture (1190, 470) is (140, 105, 71) in the picture. Reach it, with twice the tonal range you have now (the picture's ground varies by 39 levels, yours by 21): the large relief must shade the ground. Raise STONE.pale's power and take the copper out of it. If the moon's own level stops you, say what you need and try it with a switch.
2. RIMS. Every crater in the picture has a raised lip that catches the moon as a thin bright line on the side away from it and throws the crescent on the side toward it. Yours drop like a cookie cutter. Raise and light the rims, the large crater's most of all.
3. THE PITS ARE POLKA DOTS: one even field of small round pits of one size, crater floors included, close to a pattern that repeats. Vary their size widely (a power law, as the craters have), gather them in fields toward the torn edge and the lower limb, leave broad bands of smooth dust between, keep crater floors smoother than the ground round them. Clean up the sliver artifacts round the small and limb craters.
4. THE TORN CRUST, both sides, is crumbled biscuit or cork, with an arc stamp that repeats down the left band, and a grey slot shows between the right crust and the torn rock. Build it as what it is: a thick pale crust broken into a few large angular slabs with real thickness, tilted, stepping down into the hollow, their broken sides showing the crust in section (a pale top layer over darker red brown rock), with a few chips and blocks on the steps. Ten great slabs, not a thousand chips.
5. THE CRACKS. Their layout is right. The hot arm and the knot are a V cleft: one wall lit from inside, the other in shade, broken lips, and no feathered fur or spikes (at three times it reads as a glow brush stroke). The hot arm ends at the torn edge and never sticks out past it as a flat white blade. And the picture pools warm light on the rock round the knot: put that light on the stone itself, strongest on faces turned to the cleft, falling off within about 25 units, lumpy with the relief. That is light on rock, not a halo: nothing soft or round may float over the surface.
6. THE DARK SIDE is a smooth, nearly featureless dome that goes to flat brown at three times: "a CG sphere with a decal". It is the oldest surface of the planet: broad tonal variation, long vertical weathering, shallow old craters half worn away, pits and scars down to the lower limb. On the pale side the lower right limb is a clean smooth band where the picture has deep pits right down to the edge.
7. PROVE IT IN MOTION. Take real frames of a slow scroll past each side (npm run live, as CLAUDE.md shows) and look for shimmer in the pits and the cracks.
