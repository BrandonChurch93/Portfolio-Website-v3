## What must change, most important first
1. THE CRACKS ARE NEON TUBES. Every arm is one even width with a dark outline and an even soft halo, in dead straight segments: "white lightning". Open shots/world/skin/s5-match-left-beside.jpg, the picture at the left: its hot arm is THICK and molten at the knot and thins to hairlines, its thin arms come and go, and its light lies on the rock as a broad smooth warm pool, widest at the knot. So: each arm's width changes three times over along its length. The wall light comes and goes and the rock closes over it in places. The segments bend. Only the hot arm has a cream heart: the thin arms are amber and stay WARM at every brightness (they draw a neutral grey white now: blue over red 0.79 where pale gold's is 0.76, which is the palette's cap). The knot's pool fades with no edge and no speckle inside it (it ends on a hard marbled contour: a glow with an edge fails on sight). No sprays. Two thin arms still dash at the page's size: fade a line by its width on the screen.
2. THE CRUST IS GREY FOAM. The slab and its hanging chunks, and the blocks down both tears, are flat planes in a grey mauve "stucco" with soft dents. They are the planet's one stone (stone() in light.ts): warm grey brown, knapped facets with real relief, deep pits, a broken top edge. The slab's face is 63 where the picture's is 108: one lumpy pale front that takes the night's light, on a dark mass.
3. THE BAND ROUND THE PALE SIDE'S TOP is a seam: a speckled grey ring with a pale ridge line and a row of evenly spaced half dimples (its median is 131 where the picture's is about 16). Remove the ridge and the dimples. Under the sand's lip the crust is nearly all in shadow, with a few lit edges.
4. THE PALE SIDE. It lacks the picture's relief: shadowed pits with moonlit rims over the whole surface (denser by the break and the lower limb), the two mid sized craters the picture has at about (1230, 470) and (1165, 523), no dark stains painted between craters, no blocky map stains on its left half. The large crater is 20 percent too wide (its right rim is at 1225 where the picture's is 1208) and has no rim: a raised moonlit rampart on its left and lower side and a narrower shadow. The low crater's bowl is a featureless black hole with a stair stepped edge: light its lower wall and smooth the edge.
5. FACETS AND THE OUTLINE. Crumpled foil facets and rim spikes on the large crater's floor, faceted small craters, and the dark side's silhouette a flawless lathe turned arc: notch the outline a little and take the facets out.
