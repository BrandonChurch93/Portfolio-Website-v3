# Judging the core

What is being judged: the ball of light in the hollow of the hero's world and the neck under it, from which the site's stream leaves. Its place and size are picture 9's (688, 437, radius 90 in the picture) and at the page's size its face and edge match the picture's (shots/world/ref/core-large.jpg). Past that it is to be better than the picture, and Brandon's instruction for it is: "the core has to tie in with our stream that we currently have. It has to look seamless, like the stream exists there and come from it." The stream is a braided core of light with small jagged shards of dark copper and glass riding it: reference/studies/06-stream-core.jpg, and take a still of it: npm run shot -- "/lab/hero?ui=0" --scroll 1150 --t 8 --wait 2500 --out shots/world/core/review-stream.jpg.

Stills of your own: npm run look -- only:core --w 1200 --h 1200 --out shots/world/core/review-alone.jpg and at --t 9, 17 and 40, npm run look -- only:core --w 400 --h 400 --scale 1 --png --out shots/world/core/review-page.png, npm run match -- --at 560,320,260,330 --out shots/world/core/review-match, the hero as it is scrolled past the core: npm run shot -- "/lab/hero?ui=0" --scroll 500 (then 620, 760, 900) --t 6 --wait 3000 --out shots/world/core/review-hero-500.jpg, and a filmstrip: npm run strip -- "/lab/piece/world?only=core&label=0&ui=0&size=700&wide=700" --times 0,6,12,18,24,30,36,42 --cols 4 --out shots/world/core/review-strip.jpg. The rock round it is another builder's.

Judge: at the page's size is it still the picture's ball: a clear, even, fine grained face, a narrow deep orange band at a hard edge, nothing outside it, nothing white? On the hero, does the stream look as if it exists there and comes from the ball: before the stream starts is the neck whole, and once it has, is the join seamless (no step, no doubled line, no change of color)? Do the few shards round the ball read as the stream's own, give it depth, move slowly with weight, and never as clutter or dirt? At three times zoom is the grain made of something (fine sharp facets told apart by light alone), with no outlines, no terrazzo, no mush? Would it pass in a premium film?
