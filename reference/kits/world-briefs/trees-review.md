# Judging the trees

What is being judged: the belt of trees along the city's front. References: reference/concept/09-parts/09c-the-skyline.jpg, 09d, the whole picture. The kit: "Crowns are soft and full, darker inside and beneath."

Stills of your own: npm run match -- --at 60,200,1260,130 --out shots/world/trees/review-match, npm run look -- only:trees --w 1600 --h 900 --out shots/world/trees/review-alone.jpg, npm run look -- whole --out shots/world/trees/review-whole.jpg, and the whole at --scale 1 for shimmer.

Judge: does the belt sit where the picture's does? At the page's size is it a soft mass of separate crowns in autumn colors, one tree told from the next? At three times zoom is a crown a real crown with clumps, leaves, depth and a trunk, or a ball, a blob, a card? Darker inside and beneath, lit toward the moon? Do they stand on the ground with shadow? Any green, any sparkle, any visible repeat?
