## What must change, most important first
1. EVERY BUILDING HAS A LIT FACE AND A DARKER SIDE, AT THE PAGE'S SIZE. Seen square on in one tone, the city reads as stage flats. The moon is behind and to the right, so the faces the visitor sees are in its shade: what lights them is the city itself. Give the walls the city's own warm light, as real light with a direction and a fall: a strong warm spill from the streets and the lit windows that is brightest low and on faces turned to the open ground and the visitor's right, with the faces turned left a clear step darker, the tops catching the moon, and the foot of each building in its neighbour's shade. Measured on the city band at the page's size: the walls' median from 80 to about 99, the darks warm and never black (10th percentile 5 now, the picture's 21), the highlights real (99th percentile 191 now, the picture's 221).
2. DEPTH. One even brown from front to back. The front row is lit and crisp, each row behind it a step darker and softer, the last rows near silhouettes against the glow.
3. THE GOLD MARKS BLAZE. The obelisk is a matte tan shaft with a flat cream face (mean (127, 99, 64) where the picture's is (139, 116, 76) and blazing): polished gold on its head and down its lit arris. The stepped crown's reeds are a matte ochre two tone stripe: polished (METAL.gold, the night mirrored in it, a sheen that slides with the cursor's glint), as the sun disc already is.
4. WINDOWS AT THE PAGE'S SIZE are single pixel cream specks that sparkle: far away a lit clump is one soft amber patch two or three pixels across, held under pale gold, and single rooms fade to their mean.
5. THIN LIT EDGES. A beaded pale line down the right vertical edge of most buildings and along roof hips, slivers and dashes in the dark gaps between buildings, pale ticks in window fields: fade any rim thinner than a pixel and a half (common.md).
6. BOXES. Several mid rises are still one even lattice of identical windows (only:towers, about x 150 to 270, 320 to 375, 1270 to 1330, 1530 to 1640): piers, bays, windows of more than one size. Finish the saucer.
7. COST, RECONCILED. One reviewer counted 24 programs on the page against the kit's 14, and 575,000 triangles and 8 programs on only:towers against your 288,000 and 3. Say which is true and why, and bring it to your own figures.
Not yours to change: his stepped tower's limestone. A reviewer says it reads as the one cool grey mass in an amber city. It is the tower he approved, and Fable will put that to him. You may bring the city's warm spill to its lowest floors only, as to every building.
