## What must change, most important first
1. THE GREAT PIECES ARE CUT STONE. The eleven great pieces and the two boulders read as "carved handles, soap bars, crystal prisms, coffins": straight edges, ruler straight seams between beds, round bites like cheese. They are the first thing seen. Remake them as torn rock of the planet's kind: irregular masses with notches that cut back in, stepped breaks across their length, overhangs, uneven broken beds, shallow scooped scars in place of round bites, and small rubble clinging to them. The left boulder is a chunky dark lump (about 108 by 119 of the picture's pixels, its foot near 690 down) and the right one is taller (its top near 571 down, about 124 high): both near black toward the viewer, with hot orange on their undersides and edges from the core and the beam. Read them off the picture again with scripts/world/grid.mjs.
2. THE FIELD HANGS FROM THE PLANET. The band of about 150 units right under the planet's open bottom is nearly empty (3 to 4 percent covered, against 22 lower down), so the field begins as an ellipse below a dark gap. It is the band a visitor sees first. Move great pieces and their rings up close under the rock, denser there, thinning downward, as the stills of the original field have it (reference/studies/65-*).
3. LIGHT. It is a soft even tan with no deep darks and neutral grey shadows. The moon is behind the world and to the viewer's right: each piece has a crisp pale rim on its upper right and a shadow side toward the viewer that falls to warm brown black (never neutral grey: about 22, 14, 9). Pieces close together in a clump darken each other. The beam's light is warm on the faces turned to it.
4. THE BEAM IS REAL AND IT SWAYS. On the page the stream is drawn over the world, and it does not fall straight: it wanders up to about 15 units each side of the line from the core's foot (SOURCE) to the middle of the page below, and it turns in over its last stretch. Round 1 lit the field from a straight line and let pieces cross in front of the stream, where the page draws the stream over them. So: within 60 units of that line keep every piece BEHIND the beam (z less than SOURCE's z), so being drawn under it is right, and keep pieces larger than about 8 units out of a lane 40 units wide. Light from the beam is a soft warm light along that line, wide enough that the sway does not show a mismatch.
5. PIECES OF THE PLANET ARE CAUGHT BY THE BEAM. The smallest chips near the beam (the tumble kind) are the same stuff as the shards the stream carries: read src/stream/shards.ts and make those chips the same jagged shapes and give them the same look, so nobody can say where the debris ends and the stream's shards begin. They are drawn in toward the beam and down it.
6. THE WHIRL is one tidy ellipse of similar beads, "a necklace". Uneven arms: mixed sizes, clumps, gaps, stragglers, a few larger members.
7. CLEAN: bed seams that draw as dashed aliased lines of light (they will shimmer: widen them or fade them below a pixel), and the detail map showing as square texels with one grain size on every piece (scale the grain to the piece, filter it).
8. One rock: the rock's own material is being made by another builder (src/pieces/world/parts/rock/ will export it). If it exists when you finish, wear it and say so. If not, say what yours needs to match.
