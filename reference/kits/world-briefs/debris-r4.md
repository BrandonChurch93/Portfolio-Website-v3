## What must change, most important first
1. FACETED SOLIDS. Both reviewers: every great and large piece is a polyhedron of flat planes with clean straight polygon edges, which QUALITY.md fails on sight ("rock that is faceted or smooth as clay"). You have triangles to spend (279,000 drawn, one call). Put the break INTO THE GEOMETRY of every great and large piece: chipped and stepped edges along every crease, conchoidal scallops where a flake left, secondary breaks across a face so no face is one flat polygon and no edge one straight line at three times. Blocks inside a great piece meet with rubble and chips in the join, as one rock split in place, never glued boxes with a dark seam. No block is a near rectangular prism with square ends.
2. THE TWO BOULDERS, TO THE PICTURE'S OWN FORMS (shots/world/debris/r3-lboulder-beside.jpg and r3-rboulder-beside.jpg have the picture beside yours). The right one is a long shard about two to one, lying from lower left to upper right, with a jagged ridge along its upper left that burns: yours is a near cube, "a box on the right spot". The left one is one broken wedge with one large dark face, lit on its RIGHT flank toward the core: yours is a ball of nine blocks with glowing seams (a net of cracks) lit on the wrong side.
3. HAMMERED COPPER AND FINGERPRINTS. At three times the faces' relief reads as hammered copper or chocolate (an even dimple grit), and the "rings round struck places" as fingerprint or wood grain contour lines. Take the rings out (two or three at most round a real impact, never a field of them) and the dimples with them. Stone has cleavage and beds: tone that varies across a face, sharp fracture steps, strata on a broken side.
4. ONE STONE. A reviewer of the rock saw the hollow's stone, the skin and your field as "three unrelated materials", and your part no longer wears the planet's stone at all. It must: the body color under white light is stone() (light.ts), and the fine grain is the rock's own (stoneMaps in src/pieces/world/parts/rock/stone.ts gives the shared grain map: use it at your pieces' scale, without its strokes and pits, which read as cork on small pieces). A piece that drifts into the core's light must come up the same red brown as the hollow's walls beside it.
5. THE COMPOSITION. It reads as an even scatter of middling chunks. Study 65's field (reference/studies/65-the-world-rebuilt-to-picture-9-what-broke-away.jpg) and his words want a few HUGE pieces with followers, and arms that curl round the stream as a curve seen on the screen. Make the great pieces two to three times today's middling size. Thin the field toward the foot ON PURPOSE (it is as busy at 900 down as at 400 and then stops in a hard edge near 1100): large and close under the planet, smaller and sparser going down, ending in dust.
6. THE LOWER LEFT is unlit shapes on black. Either let it go to dust, or give its largest pieces a faint rim so they read as masses.
7. THE CARRIED SHARDS beside the lane are flat orange tetrahedra, one tone to a face and near one size: confetti, which is on the fail list. Each has a lit face and a dark one, their sizes vary widely, and they thin out beyond 60 units from the line.
8. WEIGHT AND COST. debris.bin is 2.0 MB: hold it there or under. Hold 300,000 triangles drawn.
9. PROVE THE MOTION in real frames at the page's size on /lab/hero scrolled to the field (npm run live): weight, families moving together, nothing popping as the stream's light arrives.
