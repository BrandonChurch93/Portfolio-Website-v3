## What is asked, and only this
1. THE RUN. Look at picture 9's lit face: it is one unbroken face, near white gold at its lower right, along the arris and the foot, falling smoothly to a deep gold at its upper left. No line, no band, no blob, no mirrored shape. Give round 13's face that run: a smooth fall across the face on the diagonal from lower right to upper left, about three to one from brightest to deepest, never to brown and never clipped over an area, the brightest part against the arris and the right corner. It is one gradient with no edge inside it. If a reviewer can point at a line or a shape on the lit face, it has failed.
2. THE DEEP FACE as deep warm satin gold with the city's glow low in it, as round 14 has it, without the streaks on its lowest courses (they show at the page's size) and without mirrored lumps.
3. THE SHEETS at three times: even, faint course lines and staggered upright joints, a few levels between neighbours. Not siding and not tiles. Quiet enough that they do not show at the page's size.
4. THE CAP stays exactly as it is.
