# Judging the land

What is being judged: the top of the planet: the desert and its dunes, the city's paved ground, the belt of dry grass. Not the buildings, trees, pyramid or spire, which are other parts or plain dark stand ins.

References: reference/concept/09-parts/09g-the-dunes.jpg, reference/concept/04-gemini-the-top-he-likes-with-a-ring-and-large-dunes.jpg (its dunes are the standard: "a sharp crest, and a hollow face in full shadow"), the whole picture.

Stills of your own: npm run match -- --at 60,200,1260,140 --out shots/world/land/review-match, npm run match -- --at 880,220,420,110 --out shots/world/land/review-match-dunes, npm run look -- only:land --w 1600 --h 900 --out shots/world/land/review-alone.jpg, npm run look -- whole --out shots/world/land/review-whole.jpg.

Judge: do the land's edge and the dunes sit where the picture has them? Are the dunes as good as picture 4's: long sharp curving crests, hollow faces in full shadow, ripples close up, sand that is pale gold in light and deep brown in shadow? Is the city's ground real paving that takes shadow and light, or one flat color? Is anything a visible repeat, a tiled texture, noise, grey, yellow, white?
