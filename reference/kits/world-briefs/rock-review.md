# Judging the torn rock

What is being judged: the FORM and LIGHT of the planet's torn rock (the roof under the land's lip, the hollow round the core, the floor, the hanging masses), as a recreation of picture 9's rock in the picture's layout. It is drawn on purpose in one plain pale material with no texture of its own, lit only by its stored light: its material comes later. Do not mark it down for having no surface texture. Do mark it down hard for anything in its form that a material could never fix.

The page shows it when the address has rock=<letter>. The letter is in the builder's report (rock-r, rock-a, rock-b: use r, a or b).

The ball of light, the pyramid, one tower, the plain dark city and land and the lines of light are not this builder's.

Judge, in this order:
1. The match, feature by feature, in the picture's pixels off (12 or fewer passes): the planet's outline, the two torn edges (near 290 to 300 and 1000 to 1040 across, both ragged), the lip (268 down at the middle), the roof's jagged lower edge (335 to 345 down over the core, hanging to about 400 at 450 to 520 and 830 to 900 across), the ring of lit slabs round the core, the floor (560 to 870 across, top 535 to 550, underside 575 to 600), the hanging masses (left to about 330, 700. right to about 1040, 690), open air under the floor.
2. Squint at the side by side: is the large pattern of bright faces and black gaps the picture's?
3. Form: any flat wall, straight edge, box, cut line? Great leaning slabs with broad clean faces and thin ragged edges, in layers with real black gaps, or cardboard cut outs, cobbles, crystals, lumps, clay, an embossed photograph?
4. Light: faces toward the core bright, soft shadow where rocks meet, bounced light in faces turned away, black where nothing reaches. The hollow lit by the core alone.
5. At three times zoom: is there more to find than the picture has, in the same character, or stair steps, facets from the mesh, mush?
