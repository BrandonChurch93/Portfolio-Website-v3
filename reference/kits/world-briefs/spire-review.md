# Judging the spire

What is being judged: the spire at the middle of the city, with its one gold ring, and its round court. References: reference/concept/09-parts/09c-the-skyline.jpg, the whole picture (its tip is at 685 across, 20 down), and the standard it must be finer than: shots/world/towers/final-alone.png, the stepped tower Brandon likes.

Stills of your own: npm run match -- --at 560,0,260,300 --out shots/world/spire/review-match, npm run look -- only:spire --w 900 --h 1500 --out shots/world/spire/review-alone.jpg, npm run look -- whole --out shots/world/spire/review-whole.jpg, and the whole at --scale 1 for sparkle.

Judge: do its outline, tip and ring sit where the picture has them? Is it the most elegant outline in the city at the page's size? Can you name what it is built of? Is the ring a real object of polished gold hanging free, tilted like Saturn's, or a plain hoop? Is it finer than the stepped tower, of the same city? Windows with depth, in clumps? Anything a plain cone or box, yellow, white, blue, sparkling?
