# Judging what broke away

What is being judged: the field of broken rock hanging under the hero's planet. It is a deliberate change from picture 9, which has little of it: "much more of it, floating close under the planet, one scene caught in the planet's gravity". Brandon's test: "Does this look like something a high-end premium movie made? Does this look like something that would be in a AAA video game? If not then it's not a good enough scene." The picture's two great boulders (about 370 to 470 and 820 to 960 across, 580 to 690 down) keep their places. The field's layout is the original plan's, which Brandon was shown and wants kept: open reference/studies/65-the-world-rebuilt-to-picture-9-what-broke-away.jpg and 65-the-world-rebuilt-to-picture-9-the-whole-page.jpg (great pieces under the planet, arms of middling pieces curling round the stream, clumps lower down, dust, thinning toward the foot). The built field must be that composition, and far better made than those stills, whose pieces were plain faceted solids.

Stills of your own: npm run look -- whole --out shots/world/debris/review-whole.jpg, npm run look -- only:debris --w 1500 --h 1100 --out shots/world/debris/review-alone.jpg, npm run match -- --at 250,520,880,248 --out shots/world/debris/review-match, a filmstrip: npm run strip -- "/lab/piece/world?size=1520&wide=1460&label=0&ui=0" --times 0,5,10,15,20,25,30,35 --cols 4 --out shots/world/debris/review-strip.jpg, and under the real page: npm run shot -- "/lab/hero?ui=0" --scroll 1100 --t 8 --wait 3000 --out shots/world/debris/review-hero.jpg.

Judge: is it one composed scene (families that read as groups, great pieces with followers, denser near the planet, thinning down, open dark between) or an even sprinkle? Is each piece real broken rock (slabs and blocks, clean faces, chipped edges) or crystals, cobbles, potatoes, clay? A real spread of sizes down to dust? Lit by the moon from behind right and by the stream's warm light, each with a lit side and a shadow side? In the filmstrip does it move slowly, with weight, families together? Is the stream's lane clear and the core unhidden? Would it pass in a premium film?
