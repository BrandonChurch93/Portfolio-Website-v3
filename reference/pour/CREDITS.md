What to notice in them:

- The stream is so hot it reads pale at its heart. Orange is only at its edges.
- It leaves the lip wide and thins as it falls.
- Spatter is irregular in shape and cooler, more orange and red, than the stream.
- Sparks are fine streaks, not dots.
- Everything near the metal is lit by it, and the light falls off fast.
